Waiting for an alarm

Keep this page open, in full-screen, and your computer awake. A desktop notification may also appear if you allow it. If the computer sleeps or the browser closes, the alarm can only fire after you wake or reopen it.

Flash across your desktop

For an alarm that flashes over other apps, install the desktop app for your computer.

Download for MacZIP download · contains a .dmg Download for WindowsZIP download · contains a 64-bit .exe

Mac: After you download the ZIP, open it in Downloads, open the .dmg inside, then drag Flash Alarm into Applications. On first launch, Control-click Flash Alarm and choose Open if macOS asks to confirm.

Windows: After you download the ZIP, open and extract it, then run the .exe installer.

On GitHub, click the matching artifact name to download the ZIP. GitHub may ask you to sign in. The apps are unsigned, so your computer may show a security prompt.
